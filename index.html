SUMCLARTECH · TALLYBOUND

Privacy Policy

Effective 4 October 2026

Tallybound is an offline survival game with optional math challenges. This policy describes the Android app published by SumClarTech.

Information stored on your device

The app stores game progress, chosen character and weapon, settings, and an optional cosmetic pack ownership cache in local app storage. SumClarTech does not receive this information. Clearing app data or uninstalling the app removes local saves.

Google Play purchases

If you choose to buy the Explorer Style Pack, Google Play processes the payment and supplies the app with purchase status and product information. The app uses that status to unlock and restore the cosmetic styles. SumClarTech does not receive your payment card details. Google may process purchase data under its own privacy policy.

Network, ads, and analytics

The game and its art run from bundled app files. The Android app does not include an advertising or analytics SDK and does not show ads. The Google Play Billing library can connect to Google Play to check prices, purchases, and entitlement status. The app does not require an account with SumClarTech.

Sharing and retention

SumClarTech does not operate a game-data server or sell game data. Local game data remains on your device until you clear it or uninstall the app. Google Play manages purchase records under its own policies.

Children

Tallybound is intended for teens and adults. Parents and guardians should supervise purchases by younger players through Google Play family settings.

Changes and contact

We may update this page when the app or its data practices change. For privacy questions, contact sumclartech@gmail.com.
